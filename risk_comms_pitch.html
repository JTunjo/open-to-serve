Analytics & WFM · Change Risk
The problem

A quiet upstream change — and the numbers you decide on are wrong before anyone raises a hand.

We change tools, metric definitions, processes, and data sources constantly. Most are fine. But some break trend comparability, trigger an escalation, or drive a wrong staffing / SLA call. Today there is no STANDARD way for the team to flag that risk before it reaches you.

Why now

What it’s costing us

  • Risks surface late or stay silent, not negligence, just no dedicated channel to raise them.
  • Flagging upward can feel like pushback, so the quiet “no” never gets said.
  • You inherit them as surprises to firefight, not decisions you could have made.
  • Once a KPI’s integrity breaks, trust in every number takes the hit.
The change

A simple, standard one-pager

  • Attached to every significant change — mandatory, lightweight, < 3 minutes to complete.
  • Three fixed questions: what must go right · what could go wrong (probability + impact) · what we recommend.
  • No new tool to learn — one template anyone on the team can use.
  • Lands on your desk as a <1-minute, comparable read.

Our ask — the CTA

Protect the messenger, reward the trust.

The team can produce the assessments, that part we own. The loop only closes with you, because the point is getting a risk in front of someone who can act on it. Here is the part that’s yours.

Your role

Three things we need from you

  1. Receive it as routine, not complaint. A flagged risk means the process is working — it’s professional practice, not resistance.
  2. Close the loop. Each note ends with a call you own — proceed / mitigate / hold. The timely decision is what creates the value.
  3. Protect the early messenger. How you react to the first uncomfortable early warning decides whether people keep raising them.
What you get back
  • Fewer operational surprises and far less firefighting.
  • Defensible KPI and forecast integrity you can stand behind.
  • A clear record of what was known, and what was decided, when.
  • A team that surfaces risk early instead of absorbing it silently.
